Who we are

About Us

Kenya's and East Africa's oldest and largest metal recycling company — purchasing, collecting, processing and recycling all forms of ferrous and non-ferrous metals for over a century.

3+ Years in operation
3 Scrap yards — Nairobi, Mombasa & Kisumu
8+ East African countries served
5+ Metal types processed

What we do

We purchase scrap metals from industrial enterprises, public entities, dealers and private individuals. We also acquire scrap from off-site demolition projects, providing expert demolition services to mines and factories — fully equipped to handle all environmental hazards including radioactive material and asbestos.

Our commitment

We are fully committed to preserving the environment and handling all materials in an environmentally responsible manner. We pride ourselves on an impeccable health and safety record throughout the Group.

Our reach

A proudly Kenyan company with a long tradition of trading across East Africa — including Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Sudan, Ethiopia, Mozambique, and the DRC. We are also a founding member of the Kenya Scrap Metal Traders Association.
